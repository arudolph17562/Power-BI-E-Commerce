# Power-BI-E-Commerce
E-Commerce Profitability and Returns Analysis
E-Commerce Profitability & Returns Analysis
Project Description

Analyzed a synthetic dataset of 2,000 e-commerce orders to identify patterns in revenue, profitability, discounts, product returns, and delivery performance. Built a three-page Power BI dashboard to communicate high-level business performance and investigate factors associated with inconsistent profitability.

Business Problem

Management identified inconsistent profitability across the business and wanted to understand:

What factors are associated with differences in profitability, and which patterns should management investigate further?

The analysis focused on category and regional performance, discounting, returns, sales channels, and delivery operations.

Tools Used
Excel — exploratory data analysis, PivotTables, descriptive statistics, correlation, and initial investigation
Power BI — data modeling, DAX measures, interactive visualizations, slicers, and dashboard development
DAX — KPIs including Total Revenue, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Return Rate, Average Order Value, and Average Delivery Days
Key Findings
The business generated approximately $250.4K in revenue and $79.4K in profit, with an overall 31.7% profit margin.
Home & Office generated the highest total revenue and profit, while Apparel had the highest profit margin at 35.6%.
Discounts showed a weak negative relationship with profit (r ≈ -0.23), suggesting higher discounts were generally associated with lower profit, although discount alone explained little of the overall variation.
Returned orders averaged approximately $21.38 profit, compared with $41.31 for non-returned orders.
Apparel had the highest category return rate at 11.5%, compared with an overall return rate of 8.1%.
The profitability impact associated with returns varied considerably by category. Home & Office showed the largest gap, with average profit of approximately $60.65 for non-returned orders versus $20.81 for returned orders.
Within Home & Office, returned Desk Organizer orders averaged approximately -$4.48 profit, identifying a specific area for further investigation.
Marketplace orders had the longest average delivery time at 4.69 days, compared with 3.68 days for Website and 3.63 days for Mobile App.
